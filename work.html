Case 01SnowflakedbtHealthcare

Eleven years of claims history off Teradata

A regional US healthcare payer had two failed migration attempts behind them and a Teradata contract renewing in eight months. Analytics was the bottleneck on every initiative in the business.

What we did

  • Three-week assessment producing a full object inventory, dependency graph and a list of the 40% of objects nobody had queried in a year
  • Automated conversion of BTEQ and stored procedures where it was safe, hand conversion for the 60 objects where it was not
  • Rebuilt the semantic layer as a tested dbt project with contracts on every mart
  • Nine-week parallel run with automated row-level reconciliation between the two platforms
  • Warehouse sizing, resource monitors and per-department credit attribution from day one

What went wrong

Three source feeds turned out to have undocumented late-arriving corrections that broke reconciliation in week five. We rebuilt those pipelines around an as-of pattern and added six weeks to the parallel run rather than cutting over on a number we could not defend. The client agreed with the call, and it is the reason the cutover was uneventful.

Case 02SalesforceDeft AIB2B SaaS

Support triage that a compliance team approved

A B2B software company in Austin was drowning in tier-one cases. Two previous AI pilots had produced convincing demos and never reached production, because nobody could agree on whether they were good enough.

What we did

  • Built an evaluation set of 320 historic cases with the support leads before writing any code
  • Agentforce routing grounded in the knowledge base and past resolved cases, with citations on every suggestion
  • Draft replies generated for the agent, never sent automatically — a human approves every outbound message
  • Guardrails for PII redaction, prompt injection and off-topic requests, tested as part of CI
  • Cost per case tracked and reported weekly alongside accuracy

What went wrong

Initial routing accuracy was 71% — well short of the 88% gate. The cause was not the model but the knowledge base, where a third of articles were stale or contradicted each other. Fixing the content took three weeks and lifted accuracy past the gate without any change to the AI at all. It is the single most common finding in our AI work.

Case 03OracleOCIJavaManufacturing

A fragile EBS estate, stabilised and moved

A mid-market manufacturer was running E-Business Suite on ageing on-premise hardware. Month-end close took eleven days, and two of the three people who understood the customisations had retired.

What we did

  • Documented the undocumented: 140 custom objects catalogued, owners identified, 30 found to be dead code
  • Lift-and-shift to OCI with a rehearsed rollback, executed over a single weekend
  • Rewrote the nine worst-performing PL/SQL packages, cutting the longest batch job from 6 hours to 22 minutes
  • Added a Spring Boot integration layer so downstream systems stopped querying EBS tables directly
  • Reporting offloaded to Snowflake, ending the 4pm slowdown analysts had accepted as normal

What went wrong

The first cutover rehearsal failed on a licensing check nobody had anticipated. We caught it in rehearsal precisely because we insisted on a full dress run three weeks before the real weekend — a step the client had initially wanted to cut for budget reasons.

Smaller engagements

Not everything is a programme

A large share of our work is measured in days. These are typical of the freelance and short-project side of the business.

4 days

Snowflake query that would not finish

A nightly aggregation had grown to nine hours and was about to miss the reporting window. Rewritten with a different clustering key and an incremental pattern — 14 minutes, and the credit spend dropped alongside it.

2 weeks

Two systems that would not talk

A Salesforce to NetSuite integration built on Platform Events with idempotency and a replay path, replacing a nightly CSV that had failed silently for a month before anyone noticed.

3 weeks

A site failing Core Web Vitals

A marketing site rebuilt on a static stack with proper image handling and schema. Largest Contentful Paint from 4.6s to 1.2s on a mid-range Android, and organic impressions up 34% over the following quarter.

6 weeks

Contracts nobody had time to read

A document extraction pilot over 4,000 supplier agreements, pulling renewal dates, notice periods and liability caps with confidence routing. Uncertain cases went to a person; the rest went to a dashboard.

Ongoing

A one-person data team, supported

A senior analyst moving into a Snowflake and dbt platform role, with two job-support sessions a week. After eleven weeks they were leading the workstream and the sessions stopped.

5 days

An APEX app instead of a spreadsheet

A quality inspection process running on a shared workbook, rebuilt as an Oracle APEX application on the database the client already licensed. Deployed in a week, still running three years later.
