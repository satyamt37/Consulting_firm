Model 01

Fixed-scope projects

You describe an outcome, we own getting there. Best for migrations, implementations and builds where the requirements can be pinned down before work starts — and where you would rather buy a result than a team.

  • Written scope, acceptance criteria and an explicit list of what is out of scope
  • Fixed price, billed against milestones you sign off
  • Two-week sprints with a demo at the end of every one
  • Change requests priced individually as they arise — never bundled into a surprise
  • Thirty-day warranty on delivered functionality after go-live
Request a project quote
How a quote is built
Step 1
Discovery call, no charge. We establish whether the scope can honestly be fixed, or whether time-and-materials serves you better.
Step 2
Assessment, one to two weeks, fixed fee. Current state, target architecture, risks and a work breakdown.
Step 3
Proposal with two or three options at different scopes and prices. You see the assumptions behind each number.
Step 4
Statement of work, kick-off within two weeks of signature.
If we cannot fix the scope, we will say so. A fixed price on vague requirements protects nobody — it just moves the argument to the end of the project.

Model 02

Dedicated pods

A standing team that works your backlog under your priorities. You get the continuity of an in-house team without the hiring cycle, and you can change direction sprint by sprint without renegotiating a contract.

  • Named people. We do not silently rotate anyone off your team.
  • Your ceremonies, your board, your definition of done
  • A tech lead who owns quality and reports to you directly
  • Three-month minimum, then rolling with 30 days' notice
  • Scale a pod up or down at each renewal without a new agreement

Model 04

Job support for working professionals

You started a role that stretches you. The stack is unfamiliar, the ticket is due Friday, and asking the same question a fourth time is not an option. Job support is a senior practitioner on a screen-share, in your evening, until you no longer need one.

How sessions run

Screen-share with one consultant who knows the technology in production, not from a course. You drive; they guide, review and explain the reasoning so it sticks.

  • 60 or 90 minute sessions
  • Evenings IST or CST, weekends available
  • Same consultant throughout

Technologies covered

The same stacks we deliver on. If we would not staff it on a client project, we do not offer support for it.

Plans

Start with a single paid session to check the fit. Most people then move to a monthly plan and taper off after eight to twelve weeks — which is the point.

  • Single session — try it once
  • Weekly — two sessions a week
  • Monthly — daily support on demand
Where we draw the line

What job support is not

We are direct about this because the term gets used loosely in this market, and because getting it wrong can cost someone their job.

  • We do not attend interviews for you or as you, in any form
  • We do not log in to an employer's systems using your credentials
  • We do not deliver your assigned work under your name while you are absent
  • We do not misrepresent anyone's experience on a CV or in a submission

What we do is teach, review, debug alongside you and explain the reasoning — so that in three months you can do the work without us. If you need someone to do the job instead of you, what you actually need is staff augmentation, and we will happily quote for that instead.

Who this works for

The first 90 days in a new role

You were hired on adjacent experience and the ramp is steeper than expected. Two sessions a week closes the gap fast.

A stack change mid-career

Moving from Oracle to Snowflake, or from Java to Salesforce. The concepts transfer; the idioms and the traps do not.

The only specialist in the building

You are the entire data team. Job support gives you the senior colleague your organisation chart does not.

A one-off deadline

A migration weekend, a release, a production incident post-mortem. Book a few sessions and stop.

Model 05

Freelance projects

Small, contained work with a clear finish line. No minimum contract value, no procurement process, no six-week sales cycle. Send the brief, get a fixed quote within one business day.

  • One integration, one dashboard, one migration script, one landing page
  • Fixed quote before we start — you approve the number, not an estimate range
  • Payment on completion for smaller pieces, milestones for anything over three weeks
  • Code, credentials and documentation handed over at the end. Everything is yours.
  • We will tell you if the job is too small to be worth our fee, and point you elsewhere
Send a freelance brief

Typical: days

A stubborn Apex bug. A Snowflake query rewritten to stop timing out. A Power BI report nobody can reproduce. A page failing Core Web Vitals.

Typical: 1–2 weeks

An API integration between two systems. A dbt model layer for one domain. A React screen. A WordPress to static migration.

Typical: 3–6 weeks

A small internal app in APEX or Laravel. An AI proof of concept over your documents. A marketing site with technical SEO done properly.

What we ask of you

A clear brief, one decision-maker, and access on day one. Freelance pricing works because the overhead is low — delays and committee reviews are what break it.

Lever 01

Duration

A three-month commitment earns a better rate than a two-week one, and a twelve-month pod better still. Discounts are written into the agreement, not offered as a negotiation trick at signature.

Lever 02

Location mix

Most clients land on blended: an onshore architect and engagement lead with an offshore engineering core. You get an accountable person in your timezone at close to offshore economics.

Lever 03

Seniority mix

Not every task needs a principal. We propose the leanest mix that will still deliver, and we show you the composition so you can challenge it.

What we will not do: quote low to win the work and recover it through change requests. Every proposal lists what is out of scope as clearly as what is in it, and change requests are priced at the same rate card you signed.
