What it is like here

The parts people ask about in the second interview

Paid honestly, reviewed twice a year

Bands are published internally. Reviews happen on a calendar, not when you threaten to leave. Offshore engineers are paid at the top of the local market because that is what retains people.

No bench, no fake CVs

We do not submit anyone for a role they cannot do, and we do not pad experience. If a project ends, you go onto internal work or training on full pay while we place you properly.

Certifications paid for

Salesforce, Snowflake, Oracle, AWS and Azure exams funded, first attempt and the retake. Study time is inside working hours, not something you do at 11pm.

Overlap, not night shifts

India teams work a 10am–8pm IST day with a fixed overlap window. Late calls happen occasionally, on a rota, with time back — not as a permanent expectation.

Small teams, real ownership

Pods of three to eight people. You will talk to the client, see the whole system and be named on the design, at every level of seniority.

Hybrid, genuinely

Two days a week in the delivery centre for team work, the rest wherever suits you. US roles are remote-first with occasional client travel.

Hiring process

Four steps, two weeks, no unpaid take-homes

We respect that you have a job already. Every stage has a purpose, and you get a decision within two working days of each one.

A note on job support and interviews. We do not accept applications from anyone offering to attend interviews on another person's behalf, and we do not provide that service to clients. It is the fastest way to end a conversation with us.

Screening call — 30 minutes

A practitioner, not a recruiter. What you have actually built, and what you want to build next.

Technical conversation — 60 minutes

A real problem from our domain, discussed and whiteboarded together. No trick algorithm puzzles.

Practical session — 90 minutes

Paired work on a small, realistic task, in your own environment. Scheduled at a time that suits you, and paid if it runs beyond the slot.

Offer conversation

Band, level, first project and growth path — discussed openly. Written offer within 48 hours of a yes.
