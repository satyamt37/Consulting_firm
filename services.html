Practice 01

Salesforce

We work on orgs at every stage — first implementation, third re-implementation, and the ones nobody wants to touch. The pattern is nearly always the same: the platform is fine, the data model drifted, and five years of quick fixes are now the bottleneck.

Where we say no: if you need a two-week Sales Cloud setup for a ten-person team, a certified freelancer will serve you better and cheaper. We are built for the messy middle and above.

Practice 02

Snowflake

We have done enough of these to know where they go wrong. It is rarely the migration — it is the six months afterwards, when three teams model the same metric differently and the monthly credit bill starts climbing without anyone owning it.

  • Assessment with a real inventory: objects, dependencies, workloads and the ones nobody uses any more
  • Parallel run and row-level reconciliation before anything is switched off
  • Cortex AI for classification, summarisation and semantic search on data already in Snowflake
  • Cost guardrails from day one, not after the first shocking invoice

Practice 03

Oracle

Oracle work splits into two very different jobs: keeping a large existing estate running, and moving parts of it somewhere better. We do both, and we are honest about which parts should not move at all.

Fusion Cloud applications

ERP, SCM and HCM implementations, phased rollouts, quarterly update readiness, OTBI and BI Publisher reporting, and functional support after go-live.

E-Business Suite

12.2 upgrades, patch cycles, custom RICEW objects, interfaces, and the application support that keeps finance calm through month-end close.

OCI

Landing zones, VCN design, lift-and-shift migrations, Autonomous Database, Exadata Cloud Service, DR topology and cost governance.

APEX applications

Replace the spreadsheet-and-email process with a proper application on the database you already pay for. Weeks, not quarters.

PL/SQL & performance

Refactoring packages nobody has touched in a decade, execution plan tuning, partitioning strategy and batch window recovery.

Analytics offload

Move reporting off the transactional database into Snowflake, keep OLTP on Oracle, and stop analysts from bringing production to its knees at 4pm.

Practice 04

AI solutions — Deft AI

Our AI practice runs under the name Deft AI. It exists because the gap between an impressive demo and a system your compliance team will sign off on is where almost every enterprise AI project dies.

  • Grounded assistants over Snowflake, Salesforce, SharePoint and document stores, with citations
  • Agentic automation for quoting, claims triage, reconciliation and tier-one support
  • Document intelligence: invoices, contracts, claims and statements into structured records
  • Classical ML where it wins — forecasting, churn, propensity, anomaly detection
  • LLMOps: evaluation sets, regression gates, prompt versioning, guardrails, spend telemetry
  • Salesforce Agentforce and Snowflake Cortex when the platform already gives you the primitives

Practice 05

Data engineering & analytics

Batch and streaming ingestion, CDC, Airflow and Dagster orchestration, idempotent reloads and alerting that names the owner.

Modelling

Dimensional, Data Vault or wide-table — chosen for your reporting reality, delivered as tested dbt code.

BI & reporting

Power BI, Tableau, Sigma and Looker. Semantic layers so a metric means one thing across every dashboard.

Governance

Catalogues, lineage, data quality tests, PII classification and access reviews that pass an audit.
