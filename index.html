Delivery method

From first call to steady state

The same five stages whether it is a Snowflake migration, an Agentforce rollout or a single freelance build. Only the duration changes.

Stage 01

Discovery call

30 minutes, no charge. We map the problem, the systems involved and what "done" looks like to you. You leave with an opinion, whether or not you hire us.

Stage 02

Assessment

One to two weeks. Current-state review, risk register, target architecture and a costed plan with options — not a single take-it-or-leave-it number.

Stage 03

Build

Two-week sprints, a demo at the end of each, and a shared board you can open any time. Changes are priced as they arise, not bundled into a surprise.

Stage 04

Cutover

Parallel runs, reconciliation, rollback plan, hypercare. We do not consider a migration finished until the old system has been dark for a full cycle.

Stage 05

Run or hand over

Managed support on an SLA, or a structured handover to your team with training and runbooks. Your call, made at the start, not the end.
