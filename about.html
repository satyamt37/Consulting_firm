Why we exist

The gap we were built to fill

There is a size of company that global systems integrators serve badly and freelancers cannot serve at all.

You need someone who has genuinely migrated a warehouse before, who understands what a Salesforce org looks like after five years of accumulated shortcuts, who can write the Java service and the dbt model and the page that ranks for it. But you cannot absorb a tier-one integrator's overhead, their six-week response cycle, or a delivery team you meet for the first time after signature.

Stratiq Cloud was set up for exactly that position. A senior architect in the US who stays on your engagement from the first call to the last. An engineering core in India that gives you scale without giving you a timezone problem. And a commercial model flexible enough to start with a single freelance task if that is all the risk you want to take today.

How we operate

Six commitments we can be held to

Values are only worth publishing if a client could catch you breaking them. These are written so you can.

01

The people you meet are the people you get

The architect in the pitch is the architect on the project. If someone has to change, we tell you before it happens and introduce their replacement properly.

02

We say no to work we should not take

If your problem is too small for our fee, outside our depth, or better solved without us, you will hear that on the first call. It costs us revenue and buys us referrals.

03

Bad news travels fast

Slippage, a wrong estimate, a discovery that changes the plan — you hear it the week it happens, not at the steering committee a month later.

04

Documentation is a deliverable

Runbooks, decision records and handover notes ship with the code, not after it. You should be able to end the engagement and still run the system.

05

No lock-in, ever

Your code, your cloud accounts, your repositories, your credentials. Our accelerators are licensed to you perpetually and their source ships with the project.

06

Change requests are priced, not weaponised

Scope changes at the rate card you already signed. We do not bid low and recover it in variations — that game is obvious to everyone and poisons the relationship.

Structure

One delivery organisation, two geographies

We are not a US front office with an unrelated offshore vendor behind it. Everyone works for the same company, on the same standards, in the same repositories, against the same definition of done.

  • Client-facing leadership, architecture and engagement management in Texas
  • Engineering, data, platform and QA depth across Hyderabad, Pune and Bengaluru
  • One code review standard and one release process, applied in both geographies
  • A daily overlap window covering the US morning and the Indian evening
  • Practice leads who own capability quality independently of any single project

Working with us safely

Security, compliance and the boring questions

The questions your procurement and risk teams will ask, answered before they ask them. If a constraint is genuinely immovable on your side, tell us on the first call and we will say honestly whether we can meet it.

Request our security pack

Contracts

Mutual NDA as standard, MSA plus statement of work per engagement, BAAs for healthcare work, and DPAs where personal data is processed.

Access

Least-privilege access to your systems, named individuals only, revoked on rotation. We work in your repositories and cloud accounts by preference.

Devices

Managed endpoints with disk encryption and MDM. We support VDI, jump hosts and locked-down client-supplied laptops where required.

Residency

Engagements can be restricted to US-based personnel, or to data that never leaves a nominated region. Agreed in writing before kick-off.

People

Background checks and right-to-work verification before anyone is placed. References available for every profile we present.

AI usage

Enterprise endpoints with training disabled. Client data is never used to improve a model, and every data path is documented.
