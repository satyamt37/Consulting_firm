Privacy notice

What we collect and why

Last updated: January 2026

Who we are
Stratiq Cloud, an IT consulting firm with its headquarters at 2591 Dallas Parkway, Suite 300, Frisco, TX 75034, United States, and delivery centres in Hyderabad, Pune and Bengaluru, India. For any privacy question, contact satyamt37@gmail.com.
What we collect
Only what you type into a form on this site: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, company, location, budget range, timeline and the message itself. Job applicants may also send a link to a CV or profile. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers on this website.
Why we collect it
To reply to your enquiry, to prepare a proposal you asked for, or to consider a job application. That is the entire purpose. We do not build marketing profiles and we do not use your details for anything you did not contact us about.
How it reaches us
Form submissions are delivered by EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, to a mailbox controlled by Stratiq Cloud. The message content passes through EmailJS and your mail provider in transit. Nothing you submit is stored in a database on this website.
Who else sees it
Our own consultants and the email delivery provider described above. We never sell your details, share them with third-party recruiters, or pass them to a partner without asking you first.
How long we keep it
Enquiries are retained for up to 24 months so we can pick up a conversation where it left off. Job applications are retained for 12 months unless you ask us to keep them longer. Client engagement records are retained for the period required by contract and tax law.
Your rights
You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will act within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under the GDPR, the CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act; we apply the same standard to everyone regardless of location.
Transfers
We operate in the United States and India, so an enquiry may be read by colleagues in either country. Where required, transfers are covered by standard contractual clauses in our client agreements.
Client data
Data we process while delivering an engagement is governed by that engagement's contract and data processing agreement, not by this website notice. In those engagements we act as a processor on the client's instructions.
