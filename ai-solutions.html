Deft AI is how we build artificial intelligence inside real enterprises — organisations with messy data, genuine auditors, and someone who has to own the system on Monday morning. Every engagement starts with an evaluation set, because a demo that works once is not a system.

Why most AI projects stall

The demo is never the hard part

Building something impressive in a fortnight is easy now. What stops it reaching production is almost always the same list — and none of it is about the model.

Nobody defined "good enough"

Without an agreed evaluation set and a target number, every review meeting becomes a matter of taste. Projects die in taste.

The data was never ready

Retrieval quality is a data engineering problem wearing an AI costume. If the source is duplicated, stale or unlabelled, no prompt saves it.

No path through risk and legal

Where does data go, what is logged, who reviews an action, how is it rolled back. Answer this in week one or lose a quarter to it later.

Cost discovered too late

Per-answer cost at pilot volume is meaningless. We model it at production volume before anyone commits to a rollout.

Our position

We would rather kill a use case in week two than in month nine

Roughly one in three AI ideas we assess should not be built — the value is too thin, the data is not there, or a rules engine does the job for a fraction of the cost. We say that out loud during discovery, and the fee for discovery is the same either way.

  • Fixed-price discovery, output yours regardless of outcome
  • Evaluation set agreed before a line of code
  • Cost per answer modelled at production volume
  • Human review designed in, not bolted on

The Deft AI method

Four stages, two decision gates

You can stop after any stage. Two of them are explicit go/no-go gates with a number attached, so the decision is not left to whoever is most enthusiastic in the room.

Stage 01 · 2 weeks

Discovery

Use-case inventory, data readiness assessment, risk and compliance constraints, value estimate per case, and a recommendation on what to pilot first.

  • Fixed price
  • Written deliverable
  • Yours to keep
Gate · Stage 02 · 4–6 weeks

Pilot

One use case built on your real data, against an evaluation set agreed before we start. Ends with measured accuracy, cost per answer and a clear go/no-go.

  • Eval set agreed up front
  • Real data, real users
  • Go/no-go number
Stage 03 · 4–8 weeks

Harden

Guardrails, fallbacks, rate and spend limits, audit logging, human review queues, monitoring and the runbook operations will actually use at 2am.

  • Security review passed
  • Rollback tested
  • Runbook handed over
Gate · Stage 04 · ongoing

Scale or hand over

Second and third use cases on the same foundation, or a structured handover to your team with training. Reviewed quarterly against the original numbers.

  • Quarterly value review
  • Cost tracked per feature
  • Exit path documented

Responsible by default

The commitments we make on every AI engagement

Your data trains nothing

We use enterprise endpoints with training disabled, and we document exactly where every byte travels. Where residency is a requirement, we design for it before we design anything else.

A person stays accountable

Any action with financial, legal or clinical consequence goes through human review. Autonomy is earned use case by use case, with evidence, never assumed.

Every answer is traceable

Inputs, retrieved context, model version, prompt version and output are logged. When someone asks why the system said that, there is an answer.

We tell you when not to use AI

If a deterministic rule, a lookup table or a better process solves it, we recommend that instead. It costs us revenue and saves you a great deal more.
